# LogHub-2.0 baseline table

Generated by `julia --project benchmarks/loghub2/sweep.jl` against the
2k-line annotated subsets fetched by `download.jl`. One row per
`(dataset, parser)` pair; metrics in percent (PA, GA, FGA, FTA, NMI,
ARI, Purity, V-measure).

Seven baseline parsers are wired in `run.jl`:

- `identity`   — every line is its own template (PA/GA floor).
- `constant`   — one global `<*>` template (template-count floor).
- `num_mask`   — `\d+ → <NUM>` plus literal-equality clustering.
- `mask`       — the full typed-slot battery in `src/PreProc/Masking.jl`.
- `drain`      — Drain3 port in `src/Parsers/Drain.jl`.
- `mask+drain` — typed-slot masking feeding Drain.
- `our_stack`  — `mask` + Drain grouping + `infer_regex` (anti-unified)
  per cluster, i.e. the thesis pipeline end-to-end.

## our_stack vs Drain

`our_stack` (mask → Drain grouping → `infer_regex` per cluster)
matches `drain` on every *clustering* metric (GA, FGA, FTA, NMI, ARI,
Purity, V) across all three datasets. The two parsers make the same
*grouping* decisions — the only difference is template *rendering*:

- `drain` emits `<*>` placeholders.
- `our_stack` emits Rust-regex templates with typed class markers
  (`<IP>`, `<INT>`, …) and `.*?` wildcards on non-aligned positions.

That raises PA for the raw `drain` on datasets whose ground-truth
uses `<*>` verbatim (Apache 69.35 %, OpenSSH 46.40 %) and drops it
on `our_stack` (where templates are regex-shaped, not `<*>`-shaped).
For downstream use — training, anomaly detection, retrieval — the
regex-shaped templates are strictly more informative.

## Parametrize knob investigation

We swept Drain's three main knobs (`depth`, `sim_th`,
`parametrize`) against the FTA gap on HDFS (ours 83.87 vs published
91.7) and OpenSSH (ours 82.35 vs published 70.5):

| knob                    | HDFS pred # | HDFS FTA% | OpenSSH pred # | OpenSSH FTA% |
|-------------------------|------------:|----------:|---------------:|-------------:|
| default                 | 17          | 83.87     | 24             | 82.35        |
| `depth = 5`             | 17          | 83.87     | 71             | 40.82        |
| `depth = 6`             | 17          | 83.87     | 123            | 24.00        |
| `sim_th = 0.3`          | 17          | 83.87     | 24             | 82.35        |
| `sim_th = 0.5`          | 17          | 83.87     | 24             | 82.35        |
| `parametrize = numeric` | 146         | 13.75     | 24             | 82.35        |
| `mask(*) + default`     | 17          | 83.87     | 24             | 82.35        |

Ground-truth counts: HDFS 14, OpenSSH 27. No single-knob tweak moves
FTA at default depth. The remaining gap vs. published Drain is
post-processing (ISSRE 2023 benchmarks apply template
canonicalisation rules on top of Drain's raw output), not the parse
tree. Covered in future work by `src/PostProc/Canonical.jl` (plan
001 Stage E′ step 3 of post-processing).

## Notes

- **Drain matches published numbers** on HDFS (NMI 99.92, GA 99.75)
  and OpenSSH (NMI 99.85, GA 78.75).
- **PA is 0 for `mask+drain` / `our_stack`** because our masks are
  more specific than LogHub's single `<*>` placeholder. The FTA /
  GA / NMI columns are unaffected.
- The `mask`-prefixed paths cost ~12 s per 2 k lines because
  `parse_line` crosses the FFI boundary once per line. A batched
  `parse_lines` Rust entry would amortise the crossing.
